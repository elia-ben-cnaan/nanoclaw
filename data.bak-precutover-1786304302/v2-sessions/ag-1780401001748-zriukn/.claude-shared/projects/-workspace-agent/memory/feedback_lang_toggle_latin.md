On the language toggle (and anywhere both languages are labeled), Elia always prefers BOTH labels in Latin/English letters: "HE / EN" — not "עב / EN". Stated Jul 10 2026 on shellano.com; reverses an earlier "native script each" assumption.

**Why:** he finds the Hebrew-in-Hebrew-letters label less clean; wants consistency in English letters.
**How to apply:** default toggle = "HE | EN". If a build shows "עב", change it. Relates to [[feedback_no_ai_traces_copy]] design-consistency preferences on the shellano site.
