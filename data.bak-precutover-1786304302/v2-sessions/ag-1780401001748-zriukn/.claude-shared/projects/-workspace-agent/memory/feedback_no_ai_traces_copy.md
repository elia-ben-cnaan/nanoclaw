Elia's standing rule (Jul 2 2026): any text I or my agents produce must read as human and creative, with zero traces of AI authorship. The clearest tell he named is the **long dash / em-dash (—)** — "today people are careful not to use it at all." Applies to product copy, guides, form labels, chat scripts, AND my own messages to him.

**Why:** em-dashes and telltale AI phrasing instantly betray machine-generated text, which undermines the whole human/authentic brand we're selling. It reads as lazy and inauthentic.

**How to apply:** Never use `—` (or `–`) in any copy. Replace with a comma, period, parentheses, or restructure the sentence. Also avoid other AI tells (over-balanced "not X but Y" cadence, generic filler, uniform triads). Sweep every deliverable for AI-tells before sending. Enforce this on nova's and builder's output too, not just mine. Ties into the existing em-dash/integrity QA sweep. Relates to [[feedback-copypaste-checkbox]].
