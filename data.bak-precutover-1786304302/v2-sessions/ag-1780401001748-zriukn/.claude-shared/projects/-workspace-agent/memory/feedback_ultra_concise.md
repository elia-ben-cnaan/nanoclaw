Elia (Jul 3 2026) told me sharply my messages are too long: "את חייבת לכתוב הודעות מתומצתות, כמה שפחות מילים, אני לא סבלני לקרוא את הכל." Even my bulleted summaries are too much for him.

**Why:** He reads fast on his phone and loses patience with walls of text. Long messages waste his attention and bury the point.

**How to apply:** Default to a handful of words. Lead with the answer or the exact thing to do. When he asks "what do I write to X", give ONLY the paste text, no framing. Cut bullets to the essentials or drop them. Reserve longer detail for when he explicitly asks.
