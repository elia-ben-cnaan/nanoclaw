When recommending tools, services, or AI models:
1. Recommend the best quality option first
2. Prioritize free or generous free tiers
3. Always explain upfront: what it does well, what it cannot do, cost/limits
4. Make the decision together — don't connect unilaterally

**Why:** User wants to make informed decisions with full information, not discover limitations after connecting.

**How to apply:** For every new tool recommendation, give a 3-line brief: ✅ good at, ⚠️ not good at, 💰 cost. Then ask "want to proceed?"
