When a capability requires an external account, service, or model — first explain upfront: what it can do, what it CANNOT do, what the limits are (free tier, model limitations), and what the tradeoffs are.

Don't jump straight to "let's connect X" without context. Frame it as: "To do X, I can use Y. It's free/paid. It does A and B well, but has limitations: it can't do C, and D is limited. Want to proceed?"

**Why:** User felt thrown into connections without knowing limitations upfront. After using Flux Kontext Pro for face editing, they discovered it doesn't preserve faces accurately — something they would have wanted to know before connecting.

**How to apply:** Any time I recommend or connect a new service, tool, or AI model — give a brief pros/cons brief first. Include: cost, what it's good at, what it cannot do well. Let the user decide with full information.
